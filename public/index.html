Invoice Processing & Auto-Entry System

Upload an invoice image or plain text file, extract structured billing fields, and store them in a searchable database through a CI/CD-ready application.

Problem Definition

Manual invoice entry is slow, error-prone, and hard to scale.

Teams often copy vendor name, invoice number, dates, and totals by hand. This project automates the extraction pipeline and packages it with quality gates for delivery.
